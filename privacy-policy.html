Introduction

Tilawa is a Quran reading, listening, and memorization (Hifz) application. Users can read the Quran, listen to recitations, track their progress, set daily goals, and use speech recognition features to support Quran memorization and recitation practice.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information responsibly.

Information Processed by the App

Tilawa may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Name entered voluntarily during onboarding
  • Reading and memorization progress
  • Daily goals (minutes or Ayat)
  • App settings such as language, theme, reading mode, and font size
  • Bookmarks
  • Notification preferences and reminder times

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to servers operated by us.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Tilawa uses the microphone only when you actively start a speech recognition feature.

Speech recognition is used to assist Quran recitation and memorization by recognizing recited verses.

Depending on your device and operating system, speech recognition may be processed by services provided by the device manufacturer or operating system provider. Tilawa does not permanently store audio recordings and does not operate any server for storing voice data.

Notifications

Tilawa may send optional reminders to help users maintain their daily Quran goals. These reminders can be disabled at any time through the device or app settings.

Internet Access

Tilawa may use an internet connection for features that require online access, such as audio playback or operating system services.

Tilawa does not sell user data, display advertising, or use personal information for marketing purposes.

Contact Feature

Tilawa includes a contact option that opens the device's email application.

When you choose to contact us, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, subject, and message.

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

Contact email: stegreif.apps@gmail.com

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Information may only be shared:

  • When you voluntarily send us an email
  • When operating system services are used for speech recognition or other device functions
  • When required by applicable law

No Advertising or Tracking

Tilawa does not display advertisements.

Tilawa does not use advertising identifiers.

Tilawa does not track users across apps, websites, or services.

Tilawa does not create user profiles.

Tilawa does not use analytics services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall the application.

You may delete your local progress and settings through app features or by uninstalling the app.

Requests regarding information voluntarily sent via email may be directed to: stegreif.apps@gmail.com

Children

Tilawa may be used by both children and adults.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of the app.

If you have questions regarding information related to a child, please contact us using the email address above.

Security

We strive to process as little personal information as possible and to keep information secure.

Because most information is stored locally on the user's device, device security also plays an important role in protecting data.
